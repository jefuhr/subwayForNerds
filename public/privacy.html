Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026

You can use Subways for Nerds without creating an account. Accounts are optional and help you move your settings between devices.

Settings and location

Favorites, themes, station filters, and widget preferences are saved on your device. Your precise location and station search text stay on your device. Saved departure boards and downloaded fleet data also stay on your device.

Optional accounts

When you sign in with Apple or Google, juliet.nyc receives a provider identifier and, when supplied, your email address. Apple’s private relay email works too. We store your linked login methods, session information, and synced settings to provide account access and synchronization. Your Apple or Google password is never sent to us.

Session tokens are stored securely on your device and are represented by hashes on our server. Provider grants retained for revocation are encrypted. Account data is used for account functionality, not advertising.

Settings files

A .nerds export includes favorites, theme, station and widget preferences, and the last selected station. It contains no account credentials. These files are readable and are not encrypted. Anyone you share one with can read those settings.

Usage and operational information

The website sends usage events, such as station views and button actions, with browser/session identifiers, device category, and referring website to juliet.nyc. These events do not include precise coordinates or station search text. Hosting services may record connection information for operation and security. Signing in also connects you to Apple or Google, whose privacy policies apply to their services.

Sign out and deletion

Signing out stops account synchronization on that device and keeps its local settings. You can delete your account in Settings. Deletion removes the active account and synced settings and invalidates its sessions. Provider revocation requests are retried if the provider is temporarily unavailable. Settings and exported files already on devices remain until you remove them. Server backups may retain deleted records until their backup retention period ends. An opaque account identifier and deletion time are retained to prevent restoring deleted accounts from older backups.

For questions, visit juliet.nyc. Provider policies: Apple and Google.
